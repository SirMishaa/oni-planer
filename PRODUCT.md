# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players of Oxygen Not Included who want to plan and optimize their colonies. They are methodical tinkerers who enjoy min-maxing resource chains. They use the tool while playing, often with the game open in another window, in a semi-dark environment. Their primary task on any screen is to quickly look up values, compare ratios, or trace production chains.

## Product Purpose

ONI Planner is a calculation and planning tool for Oxygen Not Included — similar to Factorio Planner or Satisfactory Tools. It exposes the game's internal data (elements, buildings, recipes, critters, plants, geysers, duplicants) and lets players calculate oxygen chains, production ratios, critter diets, and resource sustainability. Success means a player opens the app, finds their answer in seconds, and gets back to their colony.

## Brand Personality

Thematic, precise, playful. The tool lives in the world of ONI — it speaks to players who love the game, not to enterprise users. It should feel like a well-crafted companion app: dense with information, but visually coherent and delightful to use.

## Anti-references

- Generic game wikis (Fandom/Gamepedia): wall-of-text, no visual hierarchy, feels abandoned
- Cream SaaS dashboards (default Tailwind UI / shadcn look): too corporate, no identity
- Raw spreadsheets: cold, zero personality, doesn't honor the game's aesthetic

## Design Principles

1. **Honor the source material** — ONI has a distinctive visual language (warm ambers for hot, blues for cold/liquid, greens for biological). Reflect it; don't fight it.
2. **Data is the product** — Never hide or obscure information behind decoration. Density is a feature, not a problem to solve.
3. **Speed to answer** — Every UI decision should minimize the time between "I have a question" and "I have an answer."
4. **Earned delight** — Personality comes through in color, typography, and micro-interactions — not animations that get in the way.
5. **Context-aware** — The tool is used alongside the game. It should feel at home next to a running colony, not jarring.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Supports both dark and light themes (system preference). Color coding should never be the only differentiator for state — always pair with a label or icon.
